# São Jorge website redesign roadmap

## Foundation

- Establish Cloudflare Access administration, staging, migration checks, API contracts, structured errors, monitoring, and privacy governance.
- Make the liturgical day the canonical home experience with Brasília-aware freshness, source, revision, and last-updated metadata.

## Usability

- Add explicit calendar/date navigation, “today” recovery, preparation guidance, mobile-first service reading, loading/error/empty states, and accessible Arabic/Portuguese presentation.
- Add parish contact, location, service schedule, and attendance guidance as first-class navigation destinations.

## Content

- Replace mocked blog content with governed API-backed publishing.
- Add source/provenance and correction history to liturgical and editorial records.
- Organize catechesis around “start here”, curriculum progression, enrollment, and parish follow-up.

## Parish operations

- Add review queue, bulletin scheduling, signup intake management, retention/deletion controls, audit history, and operational health indicators.
- Add role-aware workflows for priest approval, editorial publishing, and social distribution.

## Acceptance principles

- Every public content path has source, freshness, error, and accessibility behavior.
- Every administrative mutation is authenticated, validated, auditable, reversible where practical, and tested against staging.
- No automated scraper output is treated as priest-approved content without an explicit publication transition.
